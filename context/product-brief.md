# Product Brief

## Buyer

Spanish-speaking GoHighLevel agency owners and implementers.

## Pain

Ask AI in GoHighLevel supports skills, but there are almost no ready, trustworthy skills in Spanish, and writing good ones takes time.

## Promise

Free, tested-in-format skills for common agency processes that upload in one step and never change an account on their own.

## Current Goal

Publish 8 skills, collect feedback from the GoHighLevel community, and add skills from real requests.
